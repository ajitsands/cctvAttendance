INDUSTRY PAIN POINTS

Why Traditional Biometrics Fail Modern Businesses

Fingerprint readers, RFID smartcards, and manual registers cause daily friction, time theft, and security blind spots.

⏳ Morning Queues
  • Employees line up in long queues every morning at punch clocks.
  • Creates entrance bottlenecks at main doors and turnstiles.
  • Workers lose 5 to 15 productive minutes every shift.
🪪 Proxy Punching
  • Employees forget, lose, or damage RFID access badges.
  • "Buddy Punching": Colleagues swipe cards for late or absent friends.
  • Costly badge re-issuance and endless manual HR overhead.
🦠 Hygiene & Cloud Risk
  • Physical contact scanners spread seasonal bacteria and germs.
  • Moist, dusty, or worn fingerprints frequently fail scanners.
  • Cloud vendors charge expensive recurring monthly fees.
