Executive Summary & Commercial Value

Value Proposition

Traditional biometric attendance systems rely on touch-based fingerprint readers or facial terminals installed at doorframes, resulting in morning bottlenecks, hardware wear-and-tear, maintenance costs, and hygiene concerns. Cloud-based facial recognition software introduces recurring monthly SaaS subscriptions, external network latency, and severe data privacy compliance liabilities under GCC Personal Data Protection Laws (PDPL) and GDPR.

SaNDS Lab CCTV AI Multi-Gate Face Attendance solves all these challenges through an innovative, non-intrusive paradigm: workers and visitors walk naturally through entrances while standard security CCTV cameras capture live video streams. The local Python AI engine detects and identifies faces in real-time on-premise, tracks entry/exit transitions across multi-gate pairs, compiles daily timesheets with outing break analytics, alerts security against blacklisted intruders, and syncs seamlessly with corporate HR payroll databases.

⭐ Recommended Camera Brand

UNV Cameras: Highly Compatible & Strongly Recommended

UNV (Uniview) CCTV Cameras are highly compatible and strongly recommended by SaNDS Lab for deployment with our AI face attendance system. UNV cameras deliver outstanding RTSP stream consistency, sub-second packet transit times, high-fidelity True WDR (Wide Dynamic Range), and superior optical clarity across changing ambient light conditions, ensuring peak face recognition accuracy.

Universal Multi-Brand Compatibility: While UNV Cameras are recommended for optimum performance and factory-tested stability, the SaNDS Lab system is also fully compatible with other major CCTV brands including Hikvision, Dahua, Axis Communications, Hanwha Vision, and all standard ONVIF (Profile S/G/T) compliant IP Cameras.
